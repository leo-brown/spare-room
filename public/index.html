Keep it in your circle

Create a network and share its code with family and friends. Your places and calendars are visible only to its members.

Every kind of spare space

List several guest rooms, a whole home or a camper van. Add photos, a street and a postal code, without a door number.

A calendar of possibilities

Mark nights free or busy. See what’s available around the world, then request a stay for the dates that suit you.

A welcome, confirmed

Hosts accept or decline requests. Confirmed stays block the calendar, so everyone knows where they stand.

Make yourself at home.

01 / Build the iOS app

Download the source, open ios/SpareRoom.xcodeproj in Xcode, select your Apple signing team and run on your iPhone.

02 / Bring your people

Create your account and save your recovery key. Create a network or join one using its shared code.

03 / Find a reason to visit

Add your places, share free nights and explore your network’s calendar. A stay begins with a simple request.

iOS notifications check for newly free nights and stay updates using Background App Refresh. Apple controls when checks run, so delivery can be delayed. Enable notifications and Background App Refresh on your iPhone. No push-service account is required.
